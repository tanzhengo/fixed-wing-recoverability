\section{Conclusion}
This paper presented a budgeted terminal continuation search for fixed-wing motion-primitive planning. The method combines physically normalized terminal diversity with incremental constraint rejection. On matched inputs, diversity increases finite-library witness discovery from 71.34\% to 94.63\% under a 12-candidate limit. Incremental rejection preserves these decisions while reducing the diversity method's mean continuation integration work by 87.39\%. Under a common 10,800-step limit, it enables 94.63\% recall compared with 67.76\% for cost ordering. The closed-loop suite shows a corresponding increase from 37 to 56 completed tasks out of 96, with full-library search completing 67.

The comparison demonstrates that the value of candidate diversity depends on how validation consumes the available budget. Spreading checks across maneuvers and terminating rejected rollouts address different parts of this problem. Future work will combine this allocation mechanism with wind-aware prediction, terminal periodic maneuvers, and learned candidate schedules for sensed local environments.
